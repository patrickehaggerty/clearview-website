Hiring an in-house accountant feels like the natural next step for a growing business. But when owners compare the full cost of a hire with an outsourced team, the numbers often surprise them.

## The true cost of an in-house hire

Salary is only the start. Add payroll taxes, benefits, software, training, and the time it takes to recruit and manage, and the cost of one employee climbs quickly. And one person rarely covers bookkeeping, payroll, tax, and financial strategy equally well.

## A whole team instead of one person

Outsourcing gives you access to people with different specialties for a predictable monthly fee. Your books, payroll, and reporting are handled by people who do this every day, and coverage doesn't stop when someone is on vacation or leaves.

## Better tools without the setup

An outsourced firm already knows the software. You get modern, cloud-based systems set up and connected properly, without paying for the trial and error.

## More time to run the business

The biggest return is usually time. Owners who outsource their accounting spend less time on paperwork and more time on customers, staff, and growth.
