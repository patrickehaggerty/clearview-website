Small businesses rarely start with a human resources department. Policies live in emails, onboarding happens on the fly, and benefits are handled by whoever has time. That works for a while, until the team grows or a difficult situation comes up.

## What outsourced human resources covers

An outsourced human resources partner helps with onboarding and offboarding, benefits administration, employee handbooks and policies, compliance with employment laws, and advice on employee issues. You get structure without hiring a full-time manager.

## Why it should connect to payroll

Human resources and payroll touch the same information: new hires, pay changes, benefits, and terminations. When they're handled by the same team, changes happen once and show up correctly everywhere, with fewer mistakes and less double entry.

## Signs it's time

If you have ten or more employees without written policies, have faced a termination that felt legally uncertain, or are spending your own time managing benefits, it's probably time to formalize.

Most businesses start with a review of current practices, a handbook, and a cleaner onboarding process, then add ongoing support as needed. Our team can help you decide what level of support fits your business today.
