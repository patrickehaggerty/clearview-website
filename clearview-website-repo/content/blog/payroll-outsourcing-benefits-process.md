Payroll seems simple until something goes wrong. A missed tax deposit, a wrong withholding, or a late direct deposit can lead to penalties and unhappy employees. Outsourcing payroll removes that risk.

## What you get by outsourcing

An outsourced payroll team calculates pay, withholds and deposits taxes, files returns, and prepares year-end forms. You stay in control of who gets paid and how much, without handling the details.

## Benefits owners notice after switching

Most owners expect to save time. What they often don't expect is the peace of mind of knowing filings are on time, the cleaner books that come from payroll posting correctly every run, and the easy access employees get to their own pay stubs and tax forms.

## How the process works

Each pay period, you or your managers approve hours and any changes. We run payroll, confirm deposits, handle tax payments and filings, and post everything to your accounting system. At year-end, W-2s and 1099s are prepared from records kept all year.

## How to get started

Switching is easier than most owners expect. We gather your current payroll records and employee information, set up your account, and run a parallel check before the first live payroll. Most businesses are fully moved over within one or two pay periods.
