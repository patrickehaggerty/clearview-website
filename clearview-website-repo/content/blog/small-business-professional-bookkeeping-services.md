Most business owners start out doing their own books. It makes sense early on, when there are only a few transactions and every dollar counts. But as the business grows, bookkeeping quietly turns from a small chore into a real risk. Here are five signs it's time to bring in help.

## 1. You're always behind

If you're catching up on several months of transactions at once, or only touching the books when your accountant asks, your records are working against you. Decisions made on old numbers are guesses.

## 2. Tax time is a scramble

When filing season means digging through bank statements and receipts, you're likely paying more in accountant fees and possibly missing deductions. Clean, monthly books make tax time straightforward.

## 3. You don't trust your reports

If your Profit & Loss doesn't match what you see in the bank, or categories are a mix of guesses, the reports can't help you. Reliable reports are the whole point of bookkeeping.

## 4. Cash surprises keep happening

Running short when you thought you were fine usually means income and expenses aren't being tracked closely enough. Up-to-date books show what's coming before it hits.

## 5. Your time is worth more elsewhere

Every hour spent reconciling accounts is an hour not spent on customers, staff, or growth. For most owners, handing off bookkeeping pays for itself in time alone.

Look for a team that closes your books every month, works in software you can access, and can grow with you into payroll, sales tax, and financial planning. At ClearView, bookkeeping is the foundation that everything else we do is built on.
