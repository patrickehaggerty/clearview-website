A chief financial officer helps a business plan, manage cash, and make big decisions with confidence. Most small and mid-size businesses need that kind of thinking, but can't justify a full-time executive salary. That's where an outsourced CFO comes in.

## What an outsourced CFO does

An outsourced, or fractional, CFO provides the same strategic support on a part-time basis. That typically includes budgeting, cash flow forecasting, financial reporting, KPI tracking, lender and investor preparation, and advice on pricing, hiring, and growth.

## Who needs one

Businesses that are growing quickly, planning a major investment, preparing for financing, or feeling tight on cash despite being profitable are good candidates. So are owners who simply want a clearer picture of where the business is headed.

## What it costs

Fractional CFO services are usually billed as a monthly fee based on how involved you need the CFO to be. For most businesses, the cost is a fraction of a full-time hire, and the right decisions often pay for the service many times over.
