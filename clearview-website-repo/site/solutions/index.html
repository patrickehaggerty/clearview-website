Bookkeeping & Accounting Technology

Clean, current books every month, kept in QuickBooks Online and connected to the tools your business already uses. Reconciliations, month-end close, and financial statements so you always know where you stand.

  • Monthly bookkeeping, reconciliation & month-end close
  • Catch-up bookkeeping & chart of accounts cleanup
  • QuickBooks Online setup, migration & management
  • Shopify, Bill.com, Expensify & more connected

Outsourced & Fractional CFO

CFO-level strategy and financial leadership without the full-time executive salary. Budgets, cash flow, and investor- and bank-ready reporting.

  • Monthly financial packages
  • Rolling 13-week cash flow forecasts
  • Budget development & variance analysis
  • KPI dashboards, board reporting & financing support

Payroll Outsourcing

Accurate, on-time payroll for businesses from a handful of employees to 500+, run on Paylocity by a team that knows your books.

  • Payroll processing & direct deposit
  • Federal & state payroll tax filings
  • W-2 and 1099 preparation
  • New hire reporting & compliance

Human Resources

The human resources infrastructure growing teams need, from onboarding and handbooks to benefits and compliance, connected directly to payroll.

  • Employee onboarding & offboarding
  • Benefits administration (health, 401k, FSA)
  • Handbook & policy development
  • HR compliance (FLSA, ADA, FMLA, state law)

Sales Tax

Economic nexus, destination-based rules, and local rates create real complexity in every state you sell into. We handle registration, nexus, returns, and filings nationwide.

  • Sales & use tax registration in any state
  • Multi-state filing & remittance
  • Nexus analysis for online and remote sales
  • Exemption certificates & audit support

Accounts Receivable & Payable

We manage both sides of the ledger so money moves in and out of your business on schedule.

  • Invoice creation & delivery
  • Collections follow-up & aging reports
  • Bill pay & vendor management
  • Cash flow impact reporting
